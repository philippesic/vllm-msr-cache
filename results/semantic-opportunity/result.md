# Richer representations remain an exploratory lead, not a serving win

2026-11-10 · historical · unresolved

The primary one-second prediction/value test established no semantic advantage. Exploratory quarter-second query/key features improved prediction and simulated retained value, but real cache value and native costs remain unmeasured.

## Scope

711 completed-prefix examples, task-disjoint splits; local re-encoding of retained agentic messages. A separate 54-request diagnostic observed real restores.

## Limits

Source data lacks complete cross-session timing and observation horizons. The wider-feature result is exploratory, has wide corrected intervals, and is neither scalar MSR success nor actual serving benefit.

## Decision

Keep the hypothesis and source limitations; do not build a live hybrid solely from the simulated result.
